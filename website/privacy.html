Ornamentalist Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the current Ornamentalist app for iPad and this website. The developer and data controller is Christian Mortensen. Contact: Chrismortensen1997@outlook.dk.

Data in the app

Ornamentalist does not require an account and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. The app does not itself send your drawings or usage data to the developer. Drawings, layers, imported images and settings are processed locally on your device.

Storage and deletion

Projects are stored in the app’s local documents folder, and settings may be saved as local preferences. You can delete projects in the gallery. Deleting the app and its data normally removes its local content. Exported files, device backups and copies held by other services must be managed separately. Depending on your device settings, Apple backups may include app data.

Importing and sharing

You choose which images and files to import through iPadOS. When you export or share your work, you choose the destination or recipient. Any service you choose handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy.

TestFlight and Apple

If you use TestFlight, Apple may process testing information, crash reports and feedback and share information with the developer under its own terms. This is separate from the app’s local storage. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Support emails

If you contact us, we process your email address, message and any attachments to answer your request and investigate issues. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the app. Email is handled through Microsoft Outlook. We keep support information only as long as needed to handle your request and relevant follow-up, then delete it. Please send only information relevant to your request.

This website

This site uses no first-party cookies, analytics, contact forms or external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, for hosting and security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction or deletion of personal information we hold about you, restriction of processing, or data portability, and you may object to processing. Contact us using the email above. We cannot access drawings stored locally on your device. You may lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest update.
