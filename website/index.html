Reporting a problem

Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, Ornamentalist version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot or screen recording can help. If the app provides a drawing performance report, you can include it too.

Please share only information relevant to your request. You do not need to send your artwork unless it is needed to investigate the issue and you choose to share it.

Where are my drawings stored?

Your projects are stored locally on your iPad. Export important projects to a location you control to keep an additional copy. We cannot access or recover drawings stored on your device.

Before reinstalling

Export and check your important projects first. Deleting the app and its data can remove locally stored drawings.
